Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

In short: Infusion Calculator does not ask you to create an account, does not collect personal information, and never sends the clinical values you enter to any server. All calculations run on your device. The iOS version collects no data at all; the Android version uses Google AdMob solely to display ads.

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Infusion protocols you save (drug name, volume, dose, preparation, route, notes)
  • Your quick-value lists (weight, volume, dose, flow rate, time)
  • Your theme and language preference

This data is deleted from your device when you uninstall the app. If device backup is enabled it may be included in your backup; that backup belongs to your account with the device vendor (Google or Apple), not to us.

The patient weight, dose and volume values you enter never leave your device.

Android: advertising

The Android version shows banner ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may access:

  • The advertising ID (resettable advertising identifier)
  • Device and app interaction information (ad views, ad clicks)
  • Approximate location (IP-based, country/city level)

This data is processed by Google: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent form on first launch (Google User Messaging Platform). You can decline personalised ads; every feature of the app works regardless of your choice.

To reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads: Settings → Google → Ads.

iOS: no data collected

The iOS version contains no ads, makes no network requests, and collects no data. This is declared as "Data Not Collected" on its App Store privacy label.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app includes no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. If you installed it from a store, Google Play or Apple may provide aggregated, anonymous crash statistics; this data contains no identifying information.

Children

The app is intended for healthcare staff, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Medical disclaimer

This app is a calculation tool; it does not provide medical advice and does not replace clinical judgement. Calculated values must be checked against the physician's order and your institution's protocol before use. Responsibility lies with the user.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
