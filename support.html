Frequently asked questions

The result differs from what I expected.

Check the units first: the dose unit (mg/kg/hour vs mcg/kg/minute), the total drug dose in the fluid, and the fluid volume. If you selected a protocol, make sure its concentration matches the preparation in your hands. If it still differs, report it at the address above — every report is reviewed.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Protocols, quick values and preferences are never sent to a server. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Details: privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes, every calculation works without an internet connection. On Android, only the ads need a connection.

How do I change the language?

Settings → App language. 12 languages are supported: Turkish, English, German, Spanish, Russian, French, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Korean, Japanese and Indonesian.

How do I reset quick values to defaults?

Settings → Quick values → "Reset to defaults" under the relevant field.

Can this app be used for clinical decisions?

No. It is a calculation tool; it does not provide medical advice and does not replace clinical judgement. Every result must be checked against the physician's order and your institution's protocol before use.
